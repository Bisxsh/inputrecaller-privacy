# InputRecaller Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-10_

InputRecaller is a browser extension that saves the text you type into form
fields and refills those fields on later visits. This policy explains what it
does with your information. In short: it keeps everything on your device and
sends nothing anywhere.

## What is stored

Only the text you explicitly save by clicking the extension's save icon. For
each saved value, the extension also keeps the site it came from, an identifier
that lets it match the same field again, and a last-updated time. It does not
read or store password fields, and it does not collect anything you did not
choose to save.

## Where it is stored

Entirely on your own device, in your browser's local extension storage
(`chrome.storage.local`). It is not synced to any account, and it is not sent
to the developer or to anyone else.

## What is never collected

- No personal data is transmitted anywhere.
- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
- No advertising or tracking.
- No third-party services.
- No network requests of any kind.

InputRecaller does not sell or share your information, because it never
receives it in the first place.

## Deleting your data

Open the extension's options page to see every saved value grouped by site, and
delete any entry with the Delete button. Deleting it removes it immediately.
Uninstalling the extension removes all saved values from your device.

## Changes to this policy
